# NexCell — Neo-Brutalist Club Website

> The premier Entrepreneurship Club of Rathinam Institute of Technology & Mirai School of Technology, Bangalore.

## 🎨 Theme & Palette
A vibrant **Neo-Brutalist** aesthetic with:
- **Cream Background**: `#FAF7EE` with retro dot-grid texture
- **Coral Red**: `#ff6666`
- **Electric Yellow**: `#ffff33`
- **Vivid Blue**: `#3399ff`
- **Ink Black**: `#111111`
- Heavy 3px borders, crisp offset box shadows, high contrast badges, and tactile hover feedback.

## 📄 Pages
- **`index.html`**: Launchpad with hero, stats strip, 4 pillars overview, flagship highlight, and CTA.
- **`about.html`**: The club blueprint, detailed 4 pillars, the 5-stage 0→1 pipeline, and comparison chart.
- **`events.html`**: Upcoming mystery drop teaser, Mirai 0→1 Ideathon with the winners podium, Zero to One Speaker Series with Ankur Jain, and interactive lookback gallery with lightbox modal.
- **`team.html`**: Full 39-builder directory with real photos, live instant search, and category filters (Leadership, Tech, Creative, Growth).
- **`reviews.html`**: Student & finalist testimonials, interactive review submission form with real-time card preview and star rating selector, and FAQ accordion.
- **`contact.html`**: Campus location, embedded map, direct contact info, and inquiry dispatch form.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or serve locally:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Visit `http://localhost:8000` to browse the site.
