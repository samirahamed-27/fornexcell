FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about participating in NexCell programs and joining our squad.

Any student enrolled at Rathinam Institute of Technology or Mirai School of Technology can apply during our semester recruitment drives. We welcome developers, UI/UX designers, marketing strategists, and aspiring founders from all disciplines!
Not at all! Building a startup requires design thinking, business modeling, market research, copywriting, and public speaking just as much as technical execution. Teams are formed with complementary skillsets so everyone contributes.
Mirai 0→1 is our annual national flagship ideathon. Over 150+ student innovators tackle real-world problem statements, build working prototypes with AI tooling in 24-48 hours, and defend their business models before a Shark Tank-style panel of venture founders and angel investors.
Yes! While our weekly core club meetings are on the Bangalore campus, our flagship ideathons, national hackathons, and virtual founder masterclasses are open to students across India.
